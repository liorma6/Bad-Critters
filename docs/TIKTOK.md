# Five short TikTok concepts

## 1. The spotless bench (no case spoilers)
**Hook:** ״למה הוא מנקה ספסל נקי כבר שלוש דקות?״

Production: 12–18 seconds. Use cinema mode. Follow Benny, move the lens over him and away twice, then voice his watched line. End on “מה לדעתכם הוא מסתיר?” Cut before any case event. This invites viewers to distinguish embarrassment from evidence; do not imply that Benny's reaction proves guilt.

## 2. Five neighbors, one performer
**Hook:** ״אני כל השכונה הזאת. אפילו ועד הבית.״

Production: 20–30 seconds. Alternate a close shot of the creator recording and a shot of the corresponding animal. Use one reusable greeting each; add the names in Hebrew. End with the playable game's title. No plot information needed.

## 3. Mandatory democracy
**Hook:** ״צלצלתי פעם אחת. עכשיו יש אספת דיירים.״

Production: 15 seconds. Ring the bell in daytime and film residents gathering. Layer three recorded bell responses in quick cuts, then leave a beat for Tzvika arriving. Record at ×1 for legible movement; avoid showing clues from the notebook.

## 4. The editable snack
**Hook:** ״זו לא השמדת ראיות. זו ביקורת עריכה.״

Production: 10–15 seconds. Show Amalia carrying bread, then click her harmless-secret question. Let the paper-corner line land before cutting to the creator's silent reaction. Use an exaggerated close crop, but retain the subtitle.

## 5. Suspicion isn't proof
**Hook:** ״הכי חשוד בשכונה הוא לא בהכרח האשם.״

Production: 20 seconds. Stage the harmless bench-cleaning scene and show a generic notebook note asking whether odd behavior is enough. Cut to a spoiler-free detective-rating card from a completed run. Invite viewers to play and explain their reasoning without posting culprits in comments. Do not include the actual accusation screen, essential evidence, reconstruction, or invented completion statistics.
